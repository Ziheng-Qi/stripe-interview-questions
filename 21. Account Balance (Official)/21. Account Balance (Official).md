# 21. Account Balance（Stripe 官方 prep guide 样题）

> **来源**：Stripe 发给候选人的 Engineer Team Screen 准备材料，里面的 Sample Scenario。题面、示例输出、两个 follow-up 都是原文。
> **和 10 号的关系**：10 号写的是 LC 465（最少转账次数，用回溯 / 状压 DP），对应的是这道题的 follow-up 2；**本体只要求一个能跑通的方案**，10 号没有覆盖。地里 2025 年也有一篇技术面考到 Account Balance，带"最少次数"和"审计"两个追问。

## 题目（原文）

```
At Stripe we keep track of where the money is and move money between bank accounts to
make sure their balances are not below some threshold. This is for operational and
regulatory reasons, e.g. we should have enough funds to pay out to our users, and we are
legally required to separate our users' funds from our own. This interview question is a
simplified version of a real-world problem we have here.

Let's say there are at most 500 bank accounts, some of their balances are above 100 and
some are below. How do you move money between them so that they all have at least 100?

Just to be clear we are not looking for the optimal solution, but a working one.

Example input:
AU: 80
US: 140
MX: 110
SG: 120
FR: 70

Output:
from: US, to: AU, amount: 20
from: US, to: FR, amount: 20
from: MX, to: FR, amount: 10

Potential follow ups/parts (in no specific order):
1. (Practical) If this code will be used to move millions of dollars in production, how
   would you change it? Specifically, we just eyeballed that our end goal of each
   balance >= 100 is met, how would you check that in reality? What should we do if the
   check fails?
2. (Algorithmic) Do it in the minimum number of moves. For the input data in the
   original prompt:
   from: US, to: FR, amount: 30
   from: SG, to: AU, amount: 20
```

## 思路

- **本体**：先判断总额够不够：`总额 >= 100 × 账户数`，不够就报错。高于 100 的账户当捐出方，低于 100 的当接收方，两个指针往前推，每次转 `min(还缺多少, 还能捐多少)`。按输入顺序推进，输出和官方示例**逐行一致**。
- **Follow-up 1（生产环境）**：见代码里的 `apply_and_verify`。先在副本上模拟所有转账，再检查不变量：总额不变、每个账户都 ≥ 100、没有未知账户、金额都是正数。检查不通过就**一笔都不执行**并报警，绝不执行一半。口头可以再补充：执行前重新读一遍余额（可能有并发变化）、每笔转账带 idempotency key 防止重试时重复扣款、用复式记账的 ledger、执行后做对账（reconciliation）、留 audit log。
- **Follow-up 2（最少次数）**：一般情况下求最优解是 NP-hard——"每个接收方只收一笔"本身就是装箱问题。500 个账户只能用启发式：缺口从大到小处理；如果某个捐出方能一次补齐，就在能补齐的里面选余额最小的那个（best fit），否则从余额最大的捐出方拿。在官方示例上正好得到 2 笔。规模小、需要精确解时，可以参考 10 号的回溯思路。

## 开写前要问的

- 金额都是整数吗？会不会有小数（有的话用"分"来算）？
- 总额不够时期望怎么处理：报错，还是尽量多补？
- 输出顺序有没有要求？
