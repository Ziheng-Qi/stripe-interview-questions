## Problem Breakdown:
1. Basic Input: We are given an order that includes:
   - A country (either "US" or "CA").
   - A list of items, where each item includes a product name and a quantity.
   
2. Shipping Cost Matrix: Each country has its own shipping cost matrix, which includes:
   - Products (e.g., "mouse", "laptop").
   - Quantity-based tiers for shipping cost, where shipping costs depend on the range of quantity (e.g., 0-2 units have one cost, 3+ units have another).

3. Objective:
   - Calculate the total shipping cost based on the order, considering the tiered shipping cost structure for each product and country.

### Key Challenges:
- Tiered Costs: Shipping costs depend on the quantity of the product. We had to:
  1. Match the quantity to the correct tier (e.g., a laptop costs $1000 for 1-2 units, but $900 for 3+ units).
  2. Handle unlimited upper bounds (`maxQuantity` being None for some tiers).
  
- Quantity Comparison: For each item in the order, the code needed to:
  1. Find the corresponding product in the shipping cost matrix.
  2. Determine which cost tier to apply based on the quantity.
  
### Key Solutions:
1. Parsing the Order and Matrix:
   - We looped through the products in the order and matched them with the correct product and tier in the shipping cost matrix.

3. Calculation:
   - Once the correct tier was found, we applied the cost by multiplying it with the quantity of that product and added it to the total shipping cost.
